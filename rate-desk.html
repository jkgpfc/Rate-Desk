Turning it on

Once the workflow is committed, the Action runs on its schedule, rewrites the JSON and commits it. Pages republishes within a minute or two, and this page picks it up on its next refresh without you touching anything.

Before you enable any scraper. Site terms differ. CCIL states on its own pages that it does not authorise commercial use of its website data without written permission. Do not point an automated job at a source until someone in PFC has looked at its terms. The workflow ships with automatic sources switched off and manual entry switched on, which is why it works from day one and creates no exposure.
If the light stays red. On GitHub Pages the path is relative, so data/rates.json must sit beside this file. Opening the HTML straight off your disk with a file:// address will block the fetch — serve it, or use the manual boxes below.
